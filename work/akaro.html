Akaro

Product, 2025

Akaro is a desk-based light and sound object designed to guide your day through subtle shifts in atmosphere. Inspired by the logic of a traffic signal, it translates moments of focus, pause, and rest into a system of ambient cues.

With a simple, tactile interface, Akaro lets users move seamlessly between states, turning light and sound into a quiet, continuous rhythm that supports both productivity and ease.
