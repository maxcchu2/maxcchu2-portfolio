Made on Concrete

Campaign, 2025

Made on Concrete is a conceptual project for Nude Project that celebrates the culture of street basketball. Instead of the spectacle of professional sport, it focuses on the raw, social energy of pickup games: friends gathering on the court, and the playful competition that lives between shots.

The project frames basketball not as a performance but as a mood: a place to hang out, connect, and share the rhythm of the game.
