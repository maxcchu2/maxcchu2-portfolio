Proper Company

Branding, 2026

Proper Company is a conceptual social bar based in Hong Kong, designed for young creatives seeking connection without the pressures of performative networking.

Positioned between a bar, members’ club, and creative space, it reframes social interaction as something more intentional, where conversation becomes the primary medium.
